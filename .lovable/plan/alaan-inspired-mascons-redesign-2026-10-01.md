# Alaan-inspired Mascons redesign

## Direction
Use Alaan as the visual reference without copying its brand assets or content: an airy white navigation bar, soft lilac-to-sky gradient feature areas, high-contrast black typography, compact purple calls to action, generous whitespace, and large product imagery.

## Changes
- Rework the homepage opening area into a centered, editorial composition with the existing Mascons headline, supporting copy, two actions, and a prominent fintech product visual.
- Replace the current cream/espresso palette with semantic Alaan-inspired lavender, cool blue, white, ink, and violet tokens across the site.
- Simplify decorative treatments, reduce card roundness, and make sections feel brighter and more spacious.
- Restyle navigation, statistics, client logos, solution cards, testimonials, and the closing action area so the whole homepage feels cohesive.
- Keep all existing Mascons content, routes, forms, partner logos, and fintech-only positioning unchanged.

## Validation
Check the refreshed homepage at desktop and mobile sizes, verify navigation and actions, and confirm the preview builds cleanly.
